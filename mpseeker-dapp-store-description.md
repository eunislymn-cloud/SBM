## Full Description

Now you can make music.

MPSeeker is a drum machine built for Solana by SolSynth Labs. Make beats directly on your Seeker device, mint them on-chain, and own your music forever.

Create
- 7-track drum sequencer with kicks, snares, hi-hats, claps, toms, and more
- 2 sample sounds to choose from on each track
- 3 patterns (A/B/C) with pattern chaining
- Adjustable BPM, swing, and step count (3-16 steps)
- Real-time effects: Reverb, Delay, Filter, Gain

Own
- Mint your beat as a Solana NFT
- Your wallet = your proof of creation
- Share any beat you've minted

Earn
- Original creators receive 8% royalties on every secondary sale
- Your beat sells for 100 SOL? You get paid. Forever.

Save & Export
- Save and load beats locally
- Download as WAV or MP3
- Finally, you can create your own audio for personal artwork, NFTs, streaming, and more
