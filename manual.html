🎵 Getting Started

MPSeeker is a professional drum sequencer designed for mobile and desktop. Create complex beats with 7 drum tracks, multiple patterns, and advanced features like burst effects. No account required - just start making music!

What Makes MPSeeker Special?

  • 7 Professional Drum Tracks: Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Clap, Open Hat, Rim, and Tom
  • 3 Chainable Patterns (A/B/C): Create complex song structures
  • Burst/Ratchet Effects: Add rapid-fire drum hits for dynamic rhythms
  • Per-Pattern Sound Design: Different sounds, pitch, and decay for each pattern
  • Audio Export: Download your beats as WAV or MP3 files
  • Mobile Optimized: Works perfectly on phones and tablets

⚡ Burst/Ratchet Effects

The most unique feature in MPSeeker! Burst effects create rapid-fire drum hits within a single step, perfect for snare rolls, hi-hat flourishes, and dynamic rhythms.

🖥️ Desktop
  • Right-click any active step (colored step)
  • Choose burst amount: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, or 6x
  • Step turns orange/red with burst indicator
  • Right-click again to remove burst
  • Spacebar: Start/stop playback (when not typing in fields)
📱 Mobile
  • Click the "⚡ BURST" button (turns purple)
  • Tap any active step to set burst amount
  • Choose: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, or 6x
  • Burst mode automatically turns off
  • Repeat to add more bursts

💡 Pro Tip: Burst Effects

Try adding 3x burst to snare on beat 4 for classic drum rolls, or 2x burst on hi-hats for shuffle feels. Experiment with different burst amounts on different tracks!

🎨 Pattern Creation Workflow

1. Start with the Kick

Click steps 1, 5, 9, 13 for a basic four-on-the-floor pattern, or try 1 and 7 for hip-hop style.

2. Add the Snare

Typically on steps 5 and 13 (beats 2 and 4). This creates the classic backbeat.

3. Build Hi-Hat Patterns

Add hi-hats on off-beats for drive. Try every other step or experiment with burst effects.

4. Layer Additional Sounds

Use claps to double snares, open hats for accents, rim shots for subtle percussion, and toms for fills.

5. Add Effects and Variation

Use global effects to polish your sound, then create patterns B and C for verse/chorus variations.

🔧 Advanced Features

Per-Pattern Sound Design

Each pattern (A/B/C) can have different sounds, pitch, and decay settings:

  • Sound: Different drum samples per pattern
  • Pitch: Tune drums higher or lower
  • Decay: Control how long drums ring out

Pattern Chaining

Enable "Chain" to play patterns in sequence: A → B → C → A. Perfect for creating full song structures with verse, chorus, and bridge sections.

Copy & Paste

Copy any pattern and paste to a specific pattern or ALL patterns at once. Great for creating variations quickly.

Global Effects

  • Reverb: Add spaciousness and depth
  • Delay: Create echoes and rhythmic interest
  • Filter: Shape the frequency content
  • Gain: Boost volume and add presence

💾 Saving & Exporting

Save Your Work

Use "Save" to store beats in your browser. Beats are saved locally and persist between sessions. Use "Load" to access saved beats.

Export Audio

Click "Export" to render your beat as audio:

  • WAV: High-quality, uncompressed (larger files)
  • MP3: Compressed, smaller files for sharing
  • Choose 1, 2, 4, or 8 loops for different lengths

💡 Pro Tip: Exporting

Export 2-4 loops for most uses. Single loops are great for sampling, while 8 loops work well for longer compositions or background music.

📱 Mobile Tips

  • Landscape orientation works best for the full interface
  • Burst mode is essential for mobile - use the ⚡ button
  • Two-finger scrolling helps navigate effects on smaller screens
  • Save frequently - your browser keeps your beats safe

🎯 Creative Tips

🎵 Beat-Making Techniques

  • Less is more: Start simple, add complexity gradually
  • Swing adds groove: Try 10-20% swing for human feel
  • Use burst sparingly: Burst effects are powerful - a little goes a long way
  • Experiment with step counts: 12 or 14 steps can create interesting rhythms
  • Layer patterns: Use A/B/C patterns for intro/verse/chorus structures
  • Effects enhance mood: Reverb for space, delay for movement, filter for texture

🔍 Troubleshooting

Audio Issues

If you can't hear audio, check your device volume and make sure MPSeeker has permission to play sound. On mobile, try tapping the screen first to enable audio context.

Performance

For best performance, close other browser tabs and apps. MPSeeker works best with modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).

Saving Problems

Beats are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data will remove saved beats. Always export important beats as audio files.
