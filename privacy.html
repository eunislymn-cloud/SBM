Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

🔒 The Short Version

SolSynth Labs and MPSeeker collect virtually no personal data. We don't have accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything. Your beats, your wallet, your business.

What We Don't Collect

  • No email addresses
  • No names or personal information
  • No location data
  • No cookies for advertising
  • No third-party data sharing

Basic Usage Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate (e.g., how many people press play, export beats, etc.). This helps us improve MPSeeker. This data is:

  • Anonymous - No personal identifiers
  • Aggregated - We see totals, not individual users
  • Not sold - We don't share or sell this data

What Stays on Your Device

When you use MPSeeker, the following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Saved beats - Stored in your browser's local storage
  • App preferences - Your settings stay on your device

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to mint an NFT.

Wallet Connections

When you connect a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Seed Vault):

  • Your public wallet address is used only to process transactions
  • We never have access to your private keys
  • We do not store your wallet address on any server
  • Wallet connection is handled entirely by your wallet provider

NFT Minting

When you mint a beat as an NFT:

  • Beat pattern image - Uploaded to IPFS (a public, decentralized network)
  • NFT metadata - Stored on the Solana blockchain (public)
  • Creator wallet address - Recorded on-chain as the NFT creator (public)

Blockchain data is permanent and public by design. Only mint if you're comfortable with this.

Third-Party Services

MPSeeker uses:

  • Solana blockchain - For NFT transactions
  • IPFS (via Pinata) - For storing NFT images and metadata
  • Wallet providers - Phantom, Solflare, Seed Vault (their privacy policies apply)

Children's Privacy

MPSeeker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. But honestly, our commitment to minimal data collection isn't changing.

Contact

Questions? Reach out to SolSynth Labs on X (Twitter) or through the Solana community.
